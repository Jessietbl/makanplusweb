## Introduction

MAKAN+ - A Sustainable Food Rescue Marketplace
MAKAN+ is an innovative web application designed to combat food waste and hunger in Malaysia. By bridging the gap between surplus food and community need, MAKAN+ connects restaurants and fresh produce suppliers with consumers and low-income communities, transforming waste into valuable resources.

Key Features
Real-Time Surplus Food Listings:
Enable restaurants and suppliers to post real-time surplus food offers, ensuring that food close to expiry or unsold ingredients are quickly redirected.

Efficient Supply-Demand Matching:
Utilize intelligent matching algorithms to connect available food surplus with nearby consumers and community organizations, streamlining redistribution.

Engaging Gamification:
Integrate interactive games to educate users on financial literacy and nutritional value, reinforcing healthy and budget-friendly choices.

Financial Literacy and Nutritional Education:
Empower users—particularly from the B40 community—through bite-sized, visually engaging lessons that promote informed, healthful eating decisions.

Sustainable Community Impact:
Build an inclusive food ecosystem that benefits both businesses and vulnerable populations by reducing waste, lowering food insecurity, and promoting environmental responsibility.

Impact
Reduce Food Waste:
By redirecting surplus food, the platform aims to cut down millions of metric tons of wasted food annually.

Improve Food Accessibility:
Ensuring nutritious, affordable meals reach communities in need, while simultaneously optimizing inventory management for businesses.

Empower Users Through Education:
Linking gamification with financial literacy and nutritional awareness gives users tangible skills for healthier living.

Strengthen Local Economies:
By establishing a streamlined redistribution network, MAKAN+ supports local restaurants and suppliers, driving sustainability across the food supply chain.

Technology Stack
Frontend: Next.js, React, TailwindCSS

Backend: Node.js, Express.js, Firebase Authentication

Data Management: Firestore, REST API

Payment Processing: Stripe or iPay88 (planned)

Additional Integrations: Google Gemini API for sentiment analysis, Google Maps API for route optimization, and Twilio API for notifications

How It Works
For Food Businesses:
Register on the platform, list your surplus food in real time, and manage orders efficiently through our streamlined marketplace interface.

For Consumers & B40 Communities:
Easily access and redeem surplus food listings at subsidized rates while learning through gamified modules on nutritional and financial literacy.

For Educational Engagement:
Play interactive mini-games that reward correct food-sorting decisions with coins, which can be exchanged for valuable vouchers or discounts on fresh produce.

Future Enhancements
Transactional & Logistics Features:
Integration of a seamless ordering and delivery system to further minimize food spoilage.

Advanced Sentiment Analysis:
Improved AI-driven analysis for real-time feedback to enhance user learning and engagement.

Role-Specific Dashboards:
Dedicated interfaces for restaurant owners, suppliers, and community members to better track impact and personal benefits.

Expanded Educational Modules:
More in-depth financial literacy and nutrition content tailored to diverse user needs.

With MAKAN+, we aspire to create a more sustainable, efficient, and inclusive food system in Malaysia—one that not only curbs waste but also enriches communities through education and innovative technology.
